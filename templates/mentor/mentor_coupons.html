How a coupon works

  1. Request coupons below – the mentorships team issues them.
  2. Show the QR code on your screen, or print a sheet and hand it out.
  3. Your customer scans the QR → picks your verified shop → presses Verify coupon.
  4. The key is marked used so it can never be reused, and both you and the customer get an email with all the details. You apply the discount yourself.
