How a coupon works

  1. Generate one or more coupon keys below.
  2. Show the QR code on your screen, or print a sheet and hand it out.
  3. Your customer scans the QR → lands on the verify page → presses Verify coupon.
  4. The key is marked as used in the database so it can never be reused. You apply the discount yourself.
