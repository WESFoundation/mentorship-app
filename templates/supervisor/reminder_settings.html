Automation Rules & Logic

⏱️ Smart Interval

Runs in the background every {{ settings.frequency_hours }} hours without blocking web traffic. Each user receives at most 1 email every 24 hours.

🎯 100% Exemption

Users with 100% profile completion are automatically excluded from all reminders.

🛑 Reminder Cap

Once a user receives {{ settings.max_reminders_per_user }} reminders, the system ceases sending to avoid inbox fatigue.

📝 Full Audit Trail

Every dispatch is saved to the database with completion percentage, missing fields, and email style.

Manual Immediate Run

Trigger the reminder job right now. Eligible users with incomplete profiles who haven't reached their cap or received a reminder today will be emailed.

Dynamic Email Styles

The system rotates through 5 engaging message styles to prevent repetitive emails:

🎯 Friendly Conversational and encouraging with helpful guidance.
📊 Professional Structured with bulleted checklist and progress stats.
💪 Motivational Goal-oriented push highlighting mentorship impact.
🏆 Achievement Gamified milestones emphasizing remaining steps.
🤝 Community Highlights platform community connections and visibility.
