How Mentor Ratings Are Calculated

This page explains the criteria and methodology behind the mentor rating system displayed across the platform.

Rating Overview

Each mentor is rated on a 5-star scale using 5 equally-weighted criteria

The Final Rating is the simple average of all criteria that have data. Each criterion is scored from 0 to 5 stars. The final rating is displayed on mentor cards, profile previews, and the mentor dashboard.

1

Profile Complete

Included when data is available

0-5 Stars

Measures how complete the mentor's profile is. A fully complete profile scores5 stars; partial profiles score proportionally.

How it's calculated:

  • • The system checks 31 profile fields (photo, profession, skills, education, social links, mentorship preferences, etc.)
  • • Each filled field counts as 1 point
  • • Formula: (completed_fields / total_fields) × 100 = percentage, then percentage / 20 = star rating
  • • Example: 25 of31 fields filled = 80% = 4.0 stars
Profiles with more information help mentees make informed decisions.
2

Useful Skills

Included when data is available

0-5 Stars

Measures the breadth of skills the mentor offers. More skills mean higher score, capping at5 stars for10+ skills.

How it's calculated:

  • • The system counts skills from the mentor's comma-separated skills field
  • • Formula: min(skill_count × 10, 100) = percentage, then percentage / 20 = star rating
  • • Example: 7 skills = 70% = 3.5 stars
  • • 10+ skills = 100% = 5.0 stars (maximum)
A diverse skill set indicates broader mentoring capability.
3

Mentorship Experience

Included when mentorships exist

0-5 Stars

Combines the number of completed mentorships with detailed mentee ratings across multiple criteria.

How it's calculated:

  • • Part A (50%): Completed mentorships — each completed mentorship adds1 star, up to5 (5+ mentorships = full score)
  • • Part B (50%): Average mentorship rating from mentorship reviews (including direct supervisor ratings from All Mentorships and mentee feedback)

Mentorship Ratings (each 1-5 stars):

  • • Supervisor Mentorship Rating — Rated directly per mentorship connection in All Mentorships (1-5 stars)
  • • Detailed Mentee Rating — Communication, Knowledge, Availability, and Overall Experience (each 1-5 stars)

The mentorship score feeds directly into the mentor's Mentorship Experience rating.

  • • Formula: (completed_count × 0.5) + (avg_mentorship_rating × 0.5) = star rating
  • • Example: 3 completed mentorships (1.5 stars) + avg rating 4.2 (2.1 stars) = 3.6 stars
Detailed criteria give a more accurate picture of the mentorship quality.
4

Mentorship Task Experience

Included when tasks exist

0-5 Stars

Measures task completion rate and the quality ratings mentors give to mentee work across all mentorships.

How it's calculated:

  • • Part A (40%): Task completion rate across all mentorships — (completed_tasks / total_tasks) × 100 = percentage, then / 20 = stars
  • • Part B (60%): Average task rating (1-5 stars) from TaskRating records on completed tasks
  • • Formula: (completion_stars × 0.4) + (avg_task_rating × 0.6) = star rating
  • • Example: 80% completion (4.0 stars) + 4.5 avg task rating = (1.6 + 2.7) = 4.3 stars
Task quality ratings are weighted more heavily than completion rate.
5

Admin Rating

Weight: 20% of final rating (when provided)

1-5 Stars

A direct rating provided by a supervisor based on their observation of the mentor's performance, reliability, and overall quality.

How it's calculated:

  • • Supervisors can rate each mentor from 1 to 5 stars using the star selector in the Find Mentor table
  • • Only one supervisor rating per mentor is stored (latest rating overrides previous)
  • • Conditionally included: Only counted in the final average if a supervisor has rated the mentor
  • • Example: Supervisor rates mentor 4 stars — the 5th criterion contributes 4.0 to the final average
Supervisor ratings provide a top-down quality assessment alongside peer and mentee feedback.

Simple Average of All Rated Criteria

Final Rating = Profile + Skills + Experience + Tasks + Supervisor / N
