Premium Mentorship Program

Earn while you mentor

Who is a Premium Mentor?

Premium Mentors are verified mentors with proven skill and expertise on Mentor Connect who are matched with sponsored mentees — individuals whose mentorship is supported by corporations, government programs, and institutional partnerships.

How Does It Work?

1

Corporations & Governments Sponsor Mentees

Select companies and government initiatives fund mentorship for deserving students and professionals from underserved communities.

2

You Get Matched with Sponsored Mentees

As a Premium Mentor, you may get paired with sponsored mentees.

3

Earn an Honorarium for Your Time

You may receive an honorarium for each mentorship session conducted. Your time and expertise invested are valued and rewarded.

Benefits of Being a Premium Mentor

Earn Honorarium

Get rewarded per session

Premium Badge

Stand out on the platform

Priority Matching

Get matched first

Impact Reports

Track your mentee's growth

Eligibility Criteria

  • Complete your mentor profile (100%)
  • Maintain a rating of 3.5 stars or above
  • Complete at least 1 anchor mentorship or 2 special mentorships successfully
  • Active and responsive on the platform

Scholarly Mentee Program

Stand out and get matched faster

Who is a Scholarly Mentee?

Scholarly Mentees are engaged, committed learners on Mentor Connect who demonstrate strong participation, timely task completion, and positive feedback from their mentors. A higher rating means mentors are more interested in mentoring you — helping you get matched faster and access better mentorship opportunities.

How Does Your Rating Work?

1

Your Profile & Goals

A complete profile with clear goals and interests helps mentors understand how to guide you.

2

Task Completion & Punctuality

Completing tasks on time and attending sessions consistently builds your track record.

3

Mentor Feedback on You

Your mentor's reviews of your engagement, punctuality, progress, and communication directly influence your rating.

Benefits of Being a Scholarly Mentee

Faster Matching

Mentors prefer high-rated mentees

Scholarly Badge

Stand out on the platform

Priority Access

Get matched with top mentors first

Track Growth

See your progress over time

How to Improve Your Rating

  • Complete your mentee profile (100%)
  • Complete tasks on time and attend sessions
  • Communicate actively with your mentor
  • Receive positive feedback from your mentor

Ready to stand out and get matched with the best mentors?

Complete tasks and engage with your mentors to improve your rating, then apply to become a certified Scholarly Mentee.

Tell us why you deserve the Scholarly Mentee badge. This request will be reviewed by your supervisor.
